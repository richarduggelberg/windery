Simulating wind + battery vs. electricity demand — Stockholm, Sweden (2024, hourly)

Generation mix, battery charge & demand

Probability the combination meets demand –

Wind speed data: Open-Meteo historical archive (ERA5) for Stockholm. Demand is a synthetic, illustrative profile (not real metered data) with realistic daily/weekly/seasonal shape. Base capacity generates a constant output at all times. Variable capacity dispatches to cover demand not met by wind, base capacity, and the battery, up to its installed limit, and also tops up the battery with any spare capacity once demand is covered. Battery is modeled with unlimited charge/discharge power and a simple energy cap.
